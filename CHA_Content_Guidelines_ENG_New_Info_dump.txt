Page headline | What is Hemophilia?
What is Hemophilia? (details & brief paragraphs) | Hemophilia is a rare genetic bleeding disorder that affects a person’s ability to stop bleeding. People with hemophilia can bleed longer than others after an injury or even without a known cause. While there is no cure, modern treatments allow people with hemophilia to live full, active and healthy lives. Early diagnosis, proper treatment and ongoing support are key to preventing complications and joint damage.

Topic | Your content
About VWD — description and any Khmer resources | Von Willebrand Disease is the most common inherited bleeding disorder, affecting both males and females equally. Caused by a deficiency or dysfunction of von Willebrand factor (VWF), a protein that helps blood clot. Three main types: Type 1 (mild), Type 2 (moderate), Type 3 (severe).
About other bleeding disorders — description and relevance | Rare Factor Deficiencies — deficiencies in factors I, II, V, VII, X, XI, XII, and XIII. Each requires specific diagnosis and treatment. Platelet Function Disorders — conditions where platelets don’t work properly, leading to bleeding despite normal platelet counts.
